## Prompt

> Use case: lighting-weather. Edit target: the supplied illustrated game background of Los Puntales. Correct one issue: the exterior is too brightly illuminated for a lower Penumbra district under massive structures. Keep the composition, all people and architecture, strong dark linework, three open routes (left stair to El Nudo, central workshop, right passage to verification), and the visible artificial sky panels. Lower the projected sky's brightness to a muted blue-gray and reduce its spill on walls and pavement. Make the underside of beams, walkways and building overhangs noticeably shadowed, with readable detail. Keep localized practical work light in the workshop and a few small shop windows, but reduce repeated amber light bars. Render concrete dusty and mostly matte; remove broad amber/blue floor glare while retaining a few small worn or damp patches. Aim for a covered, inhabited working city in Penumbra, not night-black: all three paths and people remain easy to distinguish at phone size. No real sunlight, hard sunbeams, long sun shadows, text, logos, UI, new objects or photorealism. Preserve 16:9 widescreen framing.
