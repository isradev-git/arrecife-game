## Prompt

> Use case: style-transfer. Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET: the Los Puntales game background. Images 2 and 3 are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: the original illustrated portraits of Sota and La Jefa. Redraw image 1 so its environment and small anonymous figures use the SAME illustration language as images 2 and 3: strong confident dark ink contour around major silhouettes and structures, broad flat color masses, angular hard-edged cel shadows, restrained highlights, selective detail on worn materials, expressive simplified folds and faces. The result must look like a background painted by the same artist as those portraits, not a detailed architecture render with an anime filter. Reduce tiny scratch marks, hatching, photographic texture, micro-lines, shiny gradients and busy repeated trim; simplify the floor and distant architecture into readable grouped shapes. Preserve the precise first-person widescreen composition and camera height of image 1, the darker Penumbra lighting, muted projected blue-gray sky, red-oxide steel, concrete, blue tarps, inhabited working city, and all three unobstructed routes: left stair to El Nudo, central workshop opening, right passage to verification. Keep the center support visible for observation. The people in image 1 remain anonymous adults and must not turn into Sota or La Jefa. Do not insert either portrait or a protagonist into the scene. No text, logos, UI, extra routes, direct sunlight, photorealism, 3D or pixel art. Keep 16:9 horizontal.

La v1, la v2, el borrador, sus prompts y los retratos originales se conservan. La v3 es una muestra candidata: la semejanza artística final requiere valoración del autor antes de integrarla o extender el estilo a otras vistas.
